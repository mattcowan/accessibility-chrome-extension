Tables

Fixture for the Tables checker (WCAG 1.3.1, Trusted Tester section 14). Each table has data-expect (the severity of its finding, or pass for none), data-expect-class (the tool's guess: data, layout or unsure) and, where it is stable, data-expect-chrome (the role in Chrome's accessibility tree). A cell with data-expect-headers lists the header texts the HTML header algorithm gives it, joined with "|".
